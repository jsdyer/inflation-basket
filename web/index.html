Sources and method

Monthly index observations are unadjusted for seasonality. Both lines start at 100 in your chosen month. A contribution is a component’s starting share multiplied by its price change. The published all-items CPI uses Statistics Canada’s own basket and linking method. Your entries stay in this browser.
